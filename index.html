How it all works

A plain-English guide to every part of the Cipher. Nothing here trades for you — it's all analysis to help you decide.

Run anything from chat

Type a command to jump straight to a feature — either a slash command or a plain word: /trade (find me a trade — full sweep, vetted, one answer), /scan all, /macro, /kb, /alerts, /log, /backup, /settings, /version, /help. Type /commands any time for the list.

Chatting & reading charts

Type a question or paste/drop a screenshot of a chart and the AI reads it using everything in its Knowledge Base. This is the "clever" part and it uses your Deep-analysis model. Tip: ask it to "go deep" for a full structured verdict.

READ — one-tap chart read

Attach a chart screenshot (or several), then tap "Give me your best read" above the chat box. You get a full swing read in one tap — bias on the 4H/Daily/Weekly, key levels, and what to do about it. Built for your phone routine: screenshot the chart, open the Cipher, attach, tap.

DIGEST — whole watchlist at once

The DIGEST header button pulls live data for every asset on your watchlist and gives a swing summary of each — bias, level, anything actionable — in one go. Perfect as a morning check before the day starts.

Coin buttons (top right)

Quick-switch the asset you're focused on (BTC, ETH, etc.) or pick "+ other". The live price shows next to them.

SCAN — finding setups

Computes the VuManChu Cipher B signals on live candles and scores each setup out of 10. Two ways to run it:

  • Scan watchlist — checks the symbols you list (full long & short read on each).
  • Scan top 200 — sweeps the 200 biggest coins. Use the Pattern dropdown to choose one setup type, or "ALL setups" to find everything in one go.

Min score filter (default 5+) hides the weak setups. A score is just a candidate — always confirm on your own chart. Hit "Ask the brain for a full verdict" on any card for the deep read.

Auto-scan in background

Tick this (in the SCAN panel) and it sweeps the top 100 coins every ~15 min while the app is open, pinging you when a new match appears. It then checks back on every match ~24h later, grades it win/loss against the real price move, and feeds that track record back into the brain. Uses free price data — no Claude cost.

Alerts & the flashing bell

Park a "wait for X" condition (e.g. price hits a level, or an A+ setup appears) and get a desktop alarm + sound when it triggers. A green pulsing bell means something fired — open it and tap Re-arm (watch again) or Delete to clear it. The watcher runs while the app is open, even in a background tab.

KB — the brain (Knowledge Base)

Everything the AI "knows" — built-in signal rules plus anything you add (paste notes, fetch a YouTube transcript, or drop a .txt). The more you teach it, the sharper its reads. This is your training, so keep it backed up (see below).

VIDEOS — learn from creators

Paste a YouTube link (optionally with the creator's name) and the AI distils the directional view — bias, levels, conviction — into a dated note in the brain. The combined summary button blends all your creators: where they agree, where they clash, and the overall lean.

MACRO — economic calendar

The US releases that move markets — CPI, jobs, Fed decisions — with live countdowns, plus Tesla earnings & delivery dates and a live TSLA price. The event dates work straight away; the live figures (inflation, jobs, Fed rate) need the one-time free relay setup (see SETUP_macro-relay.md).

Track record — does it actually work?

The auto-scan does this on its own in the background. Every setup it fires is logged at that price, then graded against the real price move 24–72h later — a WIN if it moved +2% in the called direction, a LOSS if −2%, else "chop". The running win rate, split by direction and by pattern, sits at the top of the auto-scan feed, so you can see at a glance whether the signals have an edge. It even back-fills calls that fired while the app was closed. No real money involved.

Historical backtest — prove the edge first

At the bottom of the SCAN panel, or just type backtest TSLA (or backtest watchlist) in chat. Replays years of candles and tells you in plain English whether the strategy's longs and shorts actually make money on that asset — then saves the answer to the brain so every future read on it is calibrated. Free data, no Claude cost.

Backup — never lose your training

In Settings. Export saves everything (knowledge base, patterns, trades, settings, keys) to one file; Restore brings it back on any device. Auto-backup (Chrome/Edge) writes to a file automatically on every change so it can never be lost like before. Do an Export now for an instant safe copy.

Cloud Sync — one brain, both devices

In Settings. Once set up (relay URL + your passphrase, same on phone and Mac), the app pulls the latest brain every time it opens and pushes automatically whenever it learns — so phone and desktop share one memory. Everything is encrypted with your passphrase before it leaves the device. Needs the one-time free worker setup (see SETUP_cloud-sync.md).

Self-learning

The Cipher grades its own calls: every auto-scan match is checked against the real price move at 24–72h, and each pattern's running track record is fed back into every read — so over time it leans on what's actually been winning.

Models & keeping costs down

Two models in Settings: Deep-analysis (chart reads & verdicts — put Opus here if you want the sharpest) and Routine-task (cleanups & parsing — kept on cheap Haiku). The app sends grunt work to the cheap model automatically. Leaving the app open so the auto-scan keeps tracking costs nothing — Claude is only billed when it actually reads or answers.

Share to phone

In Settings — shows a QR code to copy your API key to your iPhone so you can run the Cipher there too.

Reminder: the Cipher gives analysis and candidates, not financial advice. Always verify on your own closed-candle charts before acting, and remember single-asset concentration carries extra risk.

Discovery Lab
Fully automatic — nothing here needs you. About once a day the lab hunts for trade ideas (a local grid search over template setups plus fresh combinations from the brain), backtests every one on data it was never built on, and puts survivors straight into the scanner. Patterns that then fail in live firing bin themselves. This panel is just a window in: pause or bin anything you don't like the look of.
